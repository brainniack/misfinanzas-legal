Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel Premium? In iOS Settings: tap your name > Subscriptions > Mis Finanzas > Cancel. You can also restore purchases from the app's Premium screen.

How do I move my data to another iPhone? With iCloud on, it syncs by itself. You can also use Settings > Send backup and then Restore from a backup.

How do I record my Apple Pay purchases? In the app: Settings > Record purchases automatically, with the Shortcuts steps.

How do I delete everything? Settings > Delete all data.
