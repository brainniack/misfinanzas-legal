Privacy Policy — Mis Finanzas: Presupuesto (My Finances)

Last updated: October 10, 2026
Controller: Rdurant Solutions LLC · Contact: rainny30@gmail.com

1. What data we handle

The app stores on your device the information you enter: accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, debts, recurring payments and receipt photos. We do not run servers that receive this data, and we do not ask you to create an account or connect your bank.

2. iCloud (optional)

If iCloud is turned on, the app syncs your data between your devices using your private CloudKit database, protected by your Apple ID. We have no access to it.

3. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show ads. If you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt, it may use them to personalize ads. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and, where applicable, under “Ad privacy options” inside the app. The Premium subscription removes ads. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app queries a public exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com), sending only the code of your main currency. No personal or financial data is sent.

5. Purchases and subscription

Payments for the Premium subscription are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Backups and files you share

Backups and exports are created on your device and you share them yourself, however you choose. They are not encrypted: be careful who you share them with. When you import a bank statement, the file is read only on your device.

7. Automatic purchases (Shortcuts)

If you set up iOS Shortcuts automations, the app reads the text of messages or Apple Pay transactions that you send to it from Shortcuts, only on your device.

8. Your rights and data deletion

You can delete all your data from Settings > Delete all data and by uninstalling the app. iCloud data is managed from your device's iCloud settings.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the date of change on this page. For any question, write to rainny30@gmail.com.
